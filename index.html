Curriculum

Principle → Professional

Five Photoshop levels build on each other, supported by three bonus tracks. Open any course for the topics and the output you will produce.

LEVEL 1Design Principles & Photoshop Foundations

Topics

  • Design workflow and brief reading
  • Hierarchy, alignment, contrast, spacing, balance
  • Colour basics and font pairing
  • Photoshop workspace, canvas, resolution, file formats

You will produce

  • A correctly configured working file
  • A one-page principles checklist
LEVEL 2Core Editing & Image Control

Topics

  • Layers, groups and smart objects
  • Selections, cut-outs and layer masks
  • Crop, transform, adjustment layers, colour correction

You will produce

  • A non-destructive photo composition
LEVEL 3Typography, Layout & Social Design

Topics

  • Text styles, grids and visual rhythm
  • Flyer, poster and social-media sizes
  • Export for web, print and WhatsApp

You will produce

  • An event flyer
  • A set of social-media graphics
LEVEL 4Advanced Techniques & Compositing

Topics

  • Blend modes, filters, shadows and lighting
  • Retouching and background replacement
  • Mockups and generative tools inside Photoshop

You will produce

  • A promotional visual with a product or person mockup
LEVEL 5Professional Practice & Portfolio

Topics

  • Brand identity basics: logo use, palette, templates
  • Print versus digital delivery
  • Client briefs, revisions and file handover
  • Presenting work and building a portfolio

You will produce

  • Capstone project and short presentation
BONUS ACanva for Fast, Consistent Content

Topics

  • Templates, brand kit and magic resize
  • Social carousels, CVs, presentations
  • Team sharing and quick edits for non-designers

You will produce

  • A branded content pack
BONUS BAI Tools for Productivity

Topics

  • Prompting for ideas, captions and copy
  • AI image and generative-fill workflows
  • Fact-checking, bias, copyright and privacy

You will produce

  • A prompt library and an AI-assisted design
BONUS CDigital Literacy & Visual Communication

Topics

  • File organisation, cloud storage, backups
  • Online safety and professional communication
  • Matching message, audience and platform
  • Giving and receiving design feedback

You will produce

  • A communication brief and a personal digital toolkit
